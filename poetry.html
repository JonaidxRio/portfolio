POEM #01 • 26 AUG 2025

A Face in The Clouds

Romance Sky Musings
Under the shade of a tree, I lay on the Barandah, free. Watching the sky drift slowly, and in the clouds, I saw you. The breeze touched me running through my body and feelings blooming in my heart. Let me be the wind and you the cloud, together we'll flow, above the crowd. Let me help you move, Anywhere you would. Walk with me through every sky, only you and I — Be my forever bride...

— Jonaid Ahmed

Genesis

Written from the tranquility of the Barandah, observing clouds shapeshift into the silhouette of love and timeless companionhood.

#Barandah #Clouds #Wind

POEM #02 • 18 AUG 2026

HER

Longing Confession
Drowning in the imagination of Her, Everyone looks average except Her. Even though there are many beautiful girls but Her........, The first look of Her made me want Her. Dark bright eyes of her, Oh And the dark black hairs of Her, Ahha Like from another world. Idk I might end up being a poet by wanting Her But I still want HER, Idk if I've ever wandered in the eyes of Her But still I want HER.

— Jonaid Ahmed

Genesis

The unapologetic gravity of someone who redefines the world around you. The realization that desiring her turns one into a poet.

#Obsession #Eyes #Poet

POEM #03 • 10 NOV 2023

দুখ

অসমীয়া কবিতা Philosophy
জন্মৰ পৰা মৃত্যুলৈ কেৱল দুখঃ দুখঃ কৰিয়েই গুচি গ'ল গোটেই জীৱন । দুখঃ দুখঃ কৰিয়েই নোৱাৰিলো ধৰিব সুখ, সুখ যে আছে মানুহৰ সেৱাতে নুবুজে দুখৰ মানুহে । যদি জানিলেহেতেন মৰমতে সুখ , নাইকিয়া হ'লহেতেন এই জগতৰ পৰা দুখৰ মুখ ।

— জোনাইদ আহমেদ (Jonaid Ahmed)

Translation & Meaning

"From birth to demise, life was consumed by sorrow. In constantly chasing away grief, people fail to grasp true joy—which lies in selfless service to humanity and boundless love."

#Assamese #Philosophy #Humanity

“যদি জানিলেহেতেন মৰমতে সুখ,
