EXPEDITION LOG • OCT 2025

The Frozen Col at 4,200m

-6°C 38 km/h

We started under the starlight when frost coated the tent fly like crushed glass. The first three hours were just steady breathing and the rhythmic scrape of microspikes against hard moraine ice.

As dawn broke behind the eastern horn, the valleys below remained drowned in thick white clouds, while our ridgeline was blazing in cold, razor-sharp cobalt. You realize how trivial human anxieties are when faced with millions of tons of ancient granite.

Waypoint Data
TRAIL: North Moraine Pass
PACK WEIGHT: 14.8 kg
WATER SOURCE: Glacial Melt Runoff
SHELTER: 3-Season Wedge Tent
EXPEDITION LOG • MAY 2026

Pine Silence in the Lower Valleys

Pine Mist Solitude Run

The scent of wet cedar needles and deodar moss after a highland downpour is grounding beyond explanation. No summits to conquer today—just walking through green cathedrals where pine mist floats like silk between ancient trunks.

The mountains teach you two things: humility when looking up, and deep stillness when looking inward.

Waypoint Data
TRAIL: Deodar Ridge Trace
ELEVATION: 2,450m
DURATION: 6 hrs solo pace
COMPANION: Mountain Dog from Camp
